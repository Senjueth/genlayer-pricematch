List a product
  1. 01Buy

    The buyer pays the GEN price. It stays in escrow for the guarantee window.

  2. 02Claim

    Found it cheaper at a listed competitor? File the product page. Validators each fetch it and must agree on what it shows.

  3. 03Refund or release

    A lower price refunds the difference straight from escrow. After the window anyone releases the rest to the shop.
